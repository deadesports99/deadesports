## ⚖️ Copyright & Legal Compliance Guidelines

### 1. Garena Community Tournament Guidelines
Garena Free Fire explicitly encourages community esports tournaments and allows organizers to use official logos, map names, and in-game iconography for **tournament brackets, matchmaking screens, room schedules, and player announcements**.

### 2. Fair Use & White-Label Safety
* **Nominative Fair Use:** Free Fire logos and map graphics are used solely to accurately identify the game mode, room map, and tournament rules for registered players.
* **Multi-Tenant White-Labeling:** In the client settings, each client/organizer will display their own brand logo at the top (e.g. *"Alpha Esports presents Free Fire Tournament"*), which preserves the legal boundary between the tournament organizer and Garena.
* **Zero Asset Modification:** Logos are kept in their official aspect ratios without trademark distortion.
